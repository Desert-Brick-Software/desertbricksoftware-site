How it works

Three steps. One lasting memory.

Handwritten notes are captured individually, then arranged later — not locked into a guestbook page.

Step 1

They write.

Guests leave individual handwritten messages on a tablet during the event.

Step 2

You create.

Afterward, every message becomes its own editable piece. Move it, resize it, rotate it, add photos, and arrange everything your way.

Step 3

Keep it your way.

Save the finished design digitally or turn it into a physical keepsake such as framed wall art, prints, or books.

Why it’s different

Not just a digital guestbook.

Traditional digital guestbooks mainly collect messages. Keepscape lets the recipient create something from those messages afterward. Every handwritten message stays editable, so the couple can decide what the final memory becomes.

FAQ

A few quick answers.

Is Keepscape available now?

Not yet. We’re currently validating the concept with wedding professionals and looking for early partners before building the full platform.

Does a vendor need special hardware?

The concept is designed around compatible tablets and styluses rather than large specialized equipment.

Who creates the finished keepsake?

The couple or recipient does. Keepscape gives them the captured handwritten messages as editable elements they can arrange after the event.

Can they keep it digital?

Yes. The finished design can remain digital if they prefer.

Will physical products be available?

The planned product includes options for turning the finished design into physical keepsakes such as framed wall art, prints, and books.

Does the wedding vendor have to handle printing or shipping?

No. The goal is for Keepscape to handle that process.

How would wedding professionals make money from it?

We are currently validating partner pricing and revenue-share options with early vendors.
